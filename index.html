Month 2 — September

Hey cutie,

This will be the first entry on this website that I write to you from Spain! Madrid has been so beautiful; it's covered in greenery, and the architecture is stunning. The city is so walkable, and the weather has been so nice! I used to be someone who dreaded long walks to the point of avoiding them, but you taught me to appreciate them so much more! I definitely tend to get so caught up in work that the walk becomes a menial task to minimize, but every day you’ve shown me that the walk is actually the point. I can’t wait to take so many more with you.

I just can’t wait till November.

Work has been ramping up a lot lately for you, and I know you’re absolutely crushing it with your mitigation reports and direct client work! You make me so so proud, and I can’t keep myself from bragging about you any time something remotely related to law or immigration is brought up LMAO. It’s a problem. I just hope you’re as proud of yourself as I am of you! Or at least that you find great satisfaction in your work. And when you don’t, just remember how much you mean to your clients. They’ve told you themselves!

I know I should be soaking up every second here, and I have been, but I can’t help but imagine how much more fun it would be if you were here. But even those big things aside, those walks throughout the city, working in a café, chilling on a park bench, coming back home to cook, unwinding on the couch, brushing my teeth and getting ready for bed–I miss when we did all of these things together in New Haven.

November.

It makes me so excited that you’re learning Spanish! I know it’s a steep learning curve, especially at the beginning, but you learn so quickly (and in such an impressively organized way) that I’m sure you’ll have a basic proficiency in no time! And then we can practice together😛. I hope it’ll make you feel better to watch me flop with my Spanish when you get here LMAO.

Speaking of—I heard (/someone said in a TikTok) that in the wintertime they set up a ton of Christmas lights on the trees! I hope they’re up by the time you get here cuz that's ur favorite😛. It really is the little things. I guess you kinda have to say that since you’re little…
But you know what you’re always my favorite little thing😽😽😽

I guess the theme of this month’s message is bittersweet excitement. I’m just so excited for both of us; you’re killing it at your work, and I’m just getting started with mine! I just wish we were together doing them💔💔💔

Longing and yearning my shit rn😭

But sooner than we both know it, I’m sure all of this missing each other will have paid off come November!


Edit: Just tried Federal Café and it was fireee maybe minus the pickles. We’ll have to try it when you get here! Trying to figure out which cafés to reserve for when you’re here. That reminds me—I’m making a Madrid bucket list and I wanna talk with you to coordinate which ones you’d really wanna see so I can reserve them for when you get here! We’ll have to figure that out on a call sometime soon.

Love you! Mwah.
